Permissions & Privacy

Only What's Needed

IronFox only requests permissions needed to support features you choose to use. Your workout data is stored locally on your device.

📷 Camera Access

Used to take progress photos for comparisons and tracking. Photos can be stored inside the app or optionally saved to your device photo library.

🖼️ Photo Library Access

Used to import existing photos for progress comparisons and optionally save photos created within IronFox.

❤️ Health Data Access

Used to read HealthKit data including step count, distance, flights climbed, and calories. IronFox only accesses data you explicitly allow.

🔔 Notifications

Used for workout reminders, milestone notifications, goal alerts, and scheduled workout reminders. Can be changed or disabled anytime.
