01

Setting Up Your Profile

👤
Enter your name and body stats
On first launch, IronFox walks you through a quick setup screen. Enter your name, height, weight, and choose your unit system — imperial or metric.
🎯
Set your weekly workout goal
Pick how many days per week you want to work out. IronFox uses this to track your streak and send challenge reminders.
🔔
Enable notifications
Tap "Enable Notifications" to allow IronFox to send you daily weigh-in reminders, schedule reminders, and milestone alerts.
Tip: You can update these settings later under Profile → Settings → Units or Notifications.
02.1

Logging a Workout

➕
Open the Import tab
Tap the Import tab at the bottom of the screen. You'll see a list of workout categories — Push, Pull, Legs, Cardio, and more.
🏋️
Pick a category and exercise
Tap a category, then use the picker to select the specific exercise. IronFox will pre-fill your last saved values so you can log faster.
💾
Enter your stats and save
Fill in weight, reps, sets (or time/distance for cardio), add an optional note, then tap Save. The entry is added to your history instantly.
02.2

Entering a Workout for a Previous Day

📆
Open the Calendar Card
From the Home tab select the Calendar card. You will see a full view of the Calendar.
➕
Select the Add Workout Button when you have selected the curect Date
Tap the Add Workout button located in the detail view of the selected date. Directly under the Calendar.
💾
Enter your workout and save
Fill in weight, reps, sets (or time/distance for cardio), add an optional note, then tap Save. The entry is added to your history instantly.

Entering a Workout through a Schedule

03

Planning Your Week

📅
Open the Schedule
From the Home screen, tap the Today's Plan card (if visible) or navigate to the Calendar and tap the calendar+ icon in the top right.
✏️
Set up each day
Select a day of the week, give it a title like "Push Day", then add specific exercises using the category and workout menus.
⏰
Set a reminder time
Toggle the reminder on and pick a time. IronFox will send you a notification at the time you set so you never forget what's on the plan.
🚀
Log from Today's Plan
On the Home screen, Today's Plan shows your exercises for the current day. Tap any exercise to jump straight into logging it — no navigation needed.
04

Setting Goals

🏆
Open Goals from your Profile
Tap your profile icon in the top right of the Home screen, then tap Goals. Or tap on the Goal tab
⚖️
Set a body weight target
Enter a target weight and choose whether you're trying to lose or gain. IronFox will track your progress as a percentage and show it on the Weight card.
💪
Set exercise-specific goals
Scroll down to Workout Goals. Pick a category, select an exercise, enter a target weight or distance, and tap Save Goal. IronFox notifies you when you hit it.
Tip: Completed goals move to the Achievements section so you always have a record of what you've hit.
05

Tracking Your Weight

📊
Tap the Weight card
From the Home screen, tap the Weight card. This opens a detail view showing your weight history as a chart.
✍️
Log today's weight
Enter your current weight in the input field and tap Save. The chart updates immediately and your Home card reflects the new value.
Tip: Enable the Daily Weigh-In reminder under Settings → Notifications so IronFox nudges you each morning before you eat.
06

Progress Photos

📸
Open the Photos tab
Tap the Photos tab. You'll see two slots — Before and After — for side-by-side comparison.
📷
Take or choose a photo
Tap Camera under either slot to take a new photo, or use the Choose Photo buttons below to pick from your library or from photos saved in the app.
🗂️
Browse your saved photos
Tap "Saved Photos" to see all photos stored in IronFox, grouped by month. Tap any photo to use it in a comparison or save it to your iPhone camera roll.
Tip: Replacing a photo keeps the old one in your gallery. Tap the ✕ on a slot to clear it — the photo stays saved so you can use it again later.
07

Using the Timer

⏱️
Choose Stopwatch or Countdown
The timer on the Home screen can be set to either a stopwatch or a countdown timer. Switch between them in Settings → Home Screen → Workout Timer.
▶️
Start, lap, and stop
In stopwatch mode, press play to start. While running the play button becomes a lap button — tap it to record a split time. Press stop to pause, or stop again to reset.
🔢
Set a countdown duration
In countdown mode, tap the time display to open the full-screen view. Use the slider to set a time from 30 seconds up to 60 minutes, or use the +/− buttons to fine-tune in 30-second steps.
08

Customizing Settings

🎨
Change the background theme
Go to Settings → Background to pick a gradient color scheme for the whole app.
🏠
Show or hide Home cards
Under Settings → Home Screen, toggle which cards appear — Weight, Calories, Steps, Calendar, BMI, Measurements, Daily Planner, and more.
📐
Switch unit systems
Go to Settings → Units to switch between Imperial and Metric. IronFox will convert all your saved data automatically after confirmation.
🔒
Enable Face ID
Under Settings → Security, turn on Face ID to require authentication when opening the app. You can also set a grace period so it doesn't lock immediately.
Tip: Use Settings → Data to export your workout history as a CSV file for backup or analysis in another tool.
