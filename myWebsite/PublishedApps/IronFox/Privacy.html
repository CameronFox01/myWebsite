Privacy Policy

IronFox is built to keep your data on your device and out of anyone else's hands. Here's exactly what that means.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Overview

IronFox ("the app," "we," "us") is a workout tracking app made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and your choices around it.

IronFox does not require an account, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with third parties. Your workout data is stored locally on your device.

📴 No Accounts, No Servers

IronFox does not ask you to create an account and does not send your workout data to any server we operate. There is no sign-up, login, or email collection anywhere in the app.

💾 Where Your Data Lives

Your workouts, goals, progress photos, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup or iCloud Drive enabled on your device, this data may be included in your personal iCloud backup, the same way any other app's local data would be. That backup is encrypted and controlled entirely by your Apple ID — we do not have access to it.

If you delete the app, locally stored data may be removed unless it exists in an iCloud backup or an export you've saved elsewhere.

🔐 Permissions We Request

IronFox only requests the permissions needed for features you choose to use:

  • Camera — to take progress photos for side-by-side comparisons. Photos are stored in the app, or on your device if you choose to save them.
  • Photo Library — to import existing photos for progress comparisons, or save photos created in the app.
  • Health Data (HealthKit) — to read step count, distance, flights climbed, and active calories so they can be shown alongside your workout data. IronFox only reads data types you explicitly authorize in the Health permissions screen, and never writes to HealthKit.
  • Notifications — to send workout reminders, milestone alerts, and scheduled workout notifications. These are generated on your device and can be turned off anytime in Settings.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

🚫 No Analytics or Tracking

IronFox does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not track your activity in the app, build an advertising profile, or share usage data with anyone.

🧒 Children's Privacy

IronFox is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app requires no account and stores data locally, it does not collect identifying information from any user, including children.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
